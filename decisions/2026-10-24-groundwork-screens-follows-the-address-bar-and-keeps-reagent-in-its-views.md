# groundwork-screens follows the address bar itself, requires no wire, and keeps Reagent in its views

> The user's decision, 2026-10-24 (groundwork v4.0.0): groundwork-bridge, groundwork-access, groundwork-live-updates and groundwork-screens don't require each other; they share shapes through groundwork-steps, and the composer (groundwork) plugs them together. groundwork-screens keeps groundwork-live-updates's question store, one layer on the one below (approved). Changes in part [The page is re-frame data, from mailroom's routing](2026-10-22-the-page-is-re-frame-data.md), [The root is drawn by a function](2026-10-22-the-root-is-drawn-by-a-function.md), [parlor uses its libraries' own functions](2026-10-23-parlor-uses-its-libraries-own-functions.md), [tr is called only in views](2026-10-23-tr-is-called-only-in-views.md) and [A page has its query and language, scrolls once drawn](2026-10-23-a-page-has-its-query-and-language-scrolls-once-drawn-holds-unsaved-changes-and-loads-its-module.md).

**Decision:**

- **The routing is groundwork-screens's.** Following the address bar, moves (`:push`, `:replace`, Back and Forward), the question before leaving with `finish-move!`, a page's address, the scroll as a move tells it: groundwork-bridge's browser routing moved here, as `groundwork-screens.routes`, with its options' and move's shapes in `groundwork-screens.settings`; its refusals are `:groundwork-screens/bad-options`, `bad-move`, `bad-way`, `no-page`. groundwork-screens requires reitit-frontend for it. The api adds `make-address-of-page`, for links; the rest stays behind the events (`:groundwork-screens/go-to`, `:groundwork-screens/leave-anyway`). The history entry's number is kept in `history.state` under `"groundwork-screens"`, the scroll positions in sessionStorage under `"groundwork-screens-scroll"` (`"bridge"` and `"bridge-scroll"` before: a tab open across the deploy starts its Back positions afresh once).
- **No wire.** groundwork-screens requires groundwork-steps, not groundwork-bridge: an answer is told from a refusal by groundwork-steps's `is-error-answer?`, and its keys are `:groundwork-steps/…` (`refused`, `data`, `request-id`, `notes`, `no-answer`). The sends were handed in already, `:send-write!` and the store's send; the app makes them on its wire (groundwork's: groundwork-bridge's `send-message!`). No other function of the wire is needed: groundwork-screens opens no line.
- **The question store stays** groundwork-live-updates's, required as before.
- **Reagent in one namespace.** `groundwork-screens.views` holds everything Reagent's: `mount-root!` (the old `groundwork-screens.screen`, gone), `show-page`, `tr` with its check that a view draws (Reagent's reactive context), and the scroll after a render (the effect `:groundwork-screens/-scroll`, which a page's arrival returns). answers, pages, routes, can-i, start, texts and settings require re-frame and no Reagent; a test reads the source files and fails if one does. `groundwork-screens.api` hands `show-page`, `mount-root!` and `tr` on from the views.

**Why:** pages are drawn by groundwork-screens, so the address bar, the moves between them, the scroll once drawn and the unsaved-changes question are its job; groundwork-bridge carried them only because it came first, and groundwork-screens required the whole wire for them and for one function, `is-error-answer?`. With the routing here and the shapes in groundwork-steps, groundwork-screens requires no sibling but the store below it. Reagent in one namespace keeps the logic (what is held, what a move does, what may be done) readable and testable as re-frame data alone, and leaves one place to change if the drawing does.

**Instead of:**
- the routing staying in groundwork-bridge, handed to groundwork-screens as functions at the start: four functions written out in every app for a job nothing but groundwork-screens does
- the routing as a library of its own: one user, groundwork-screens, and its options are groundwork-screens's settings already
- the old history and sessionStorage keys kept: they would name a library that no longer does this
- `tr` staying in the api with its Reagent check: the api would require Reagent, and the rule "one namespace" would have an exception
